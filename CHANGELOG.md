## 1.0.0 (2026-11-13)

First release.

- The lighthouse keeper's logbook: a night is three hours of the real clock; you choose how the lamp
  burns (early, at dark, late or not at all) and the words you write, and the patterns in the log
  predict the storms and the ships. No combat, nothing to lose.
- Away, a relief hand keeps the lamp and writes plain entries; letters come from the relief hand.
  Storms foreseen are counted, a nudge helps, the clock keeps on while you compose.
- A safe save (written beside, renamed when whole), a new log (R), and `LEDGER.LOG`, a plain-text
  diary you can `TYPE`.
- VT100 (line drawing, scroll region, dim, bold, blink, reverse), VT52 and ADM-3A; without a clock
  device every start is the next night.
